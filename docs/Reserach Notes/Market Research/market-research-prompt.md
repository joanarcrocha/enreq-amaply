# Context
FAIR Software Solutions is a startup that aims to develop software solutions 
geared towards facilitating the distribution and sale of food products, in the 
context of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, adopted by all 
United Nations Member States in 2015, which comprises 17 Sustainable 
Development Goals (https://ods.pt/ods/).  
FAIR Software Solutions has recently identified a business opportunity in the 
AMAP/CSA field. For this reason, it is considering developing a solution for 
this market and is looking for a highly motivated team to specify the functional 
and non-functional requirements for this software solution, covering all of its 
processes.  
An AMAP1 (Association for the Maintenance of Local Farming) 
and CSA (Community Supported Agriculture) are two names for a common 
goal: consumers and producers united by growing healthy food and by a more 
dignified life for those who produce what feeds us in a sustainable, ecosystem
1https://amap.movingcause.org/ regenerating way. An AMAP or CSA, is formed by a group of consumers who 
actively and directly support one or more farmers and producers, ensuring the 
distribution of their produce.
