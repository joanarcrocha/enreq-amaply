# ENGREQ Interview Questions and Answers

## Questions for Producers

### 1. Payments and invoicing
**Question:** How should payments and invoicing work: should co-producers pay through amaply or externally, should payment go to you or the AMAP, and who should issue invoices?

**Answer:** Both payments through amaply and external payments are acceptable. The process should be made easier, with one combined payment if possible. Producers need access to the money before delivery to finance production, so the platform should not hold payments until delivery.

Each AMAP can decide whether to accept payments through the platform or externally. The interview did not specify who should receive the payments or issue invoices.

### 2. Seasonal shortages, missing products and quality problems
**Question:** How do you handle seasonal shortages, missing products and quality problems? When should you offer replacements, price adjustments or refunds?

**Answer:** The producer would like to choose how to resolve a missing delivery through the portal: provide a replacement product, issue a refund, or give the co-producer credit (a balance) on the platform. For example, if a €40 chicken order cannot be delivered because a fox ate the chickens, the producer should be able to choose among these options. No specific procedure for quality problems was described.

### 3. Subscription changes
**Question:** Can co-producers join, change, cancel, transfer or renew a subscription during a commitment? What conditions or deadlines should apply?

**Answer:** A co-producer can cancel or pause a subscription only if AMAP management approves. Specific rules for joining, changing, transferring, renewing and deadlines were not provided.

### 4. Producer and coordinator responsibilities
**Question:** Which decisions should belong to you, and which to AMAP coordinators, for example, admitting members, managing waiting lists, setting schedules and changing operating rules?

**Answer:** AMAP management can decide the full delivery calendar. If a scheduled date is unsuitable, the AMAP can discuss it internally. Producers cannot decide which new customers are admitted. No specific waiting-list priority or procedure for changing operating rules was given.

### 5. Collection arrangements and notifications
**Question:** How should absences, collection by another person and uncollected baskets be handled? Which notifications do you need, and through which channels?

**Answer:** When a co-producer knows they cannot collect an order, the platform should prompt them with alternatives, such as having another person collect it, arranging collection by another institution, or giving the products to other co-producers. The platform does not need to manage baskets that remain uncollected.

Producers should receive notifications, for example, one day before delivery. Identity verification at pickup is not considered particularly necessary, though the AMAP might decide otherwise. No notification channel was specified.

### 6. Additional roles and internationalization
**Question:** Would you also use amaply as a co-producer? Which countries, languages, currencies and payment methods do you need at launch?

**Answer:** The producer responded, “Does not matter.” No specific launch requirements were identified in this answer.

## Questions for Co-Producers

### 1. Payments and invoicing
**Question:** How would you prefer to pay and receive invoices? Do you need one combined payment for all producers, and which payment methods are essential?

**Answer:** The co-producer would prefer to make one payment covering all their AMAPs, but not pay for the entire season at once. The AMAP would distribute the money. MB Way and bank transfers are current payment methods; cash is not normally used. Apple Pay and PayPal could be interesting options. These comments reflect current practices rather than a definitive list of preferred methods. No invoice preference was recorded.

### 2. Seasonal variations and product quality
**Question:** What would you expect if quantities vary seasonally, a product is missing or something arrives in poor condition?

**Answer:** For most products, especially vegetables, the co-producer prefers a seasonal basket without choosing every individual item. Other products, such as eggs, milk and bread, could be selected individually. No specific response about shortages, poor quality, replacements or refunds was recorded.

### 3. Subscription flexibility
**Question:** When would you need to change, cancel or transfer a subscription? How would you prefer to renew it?

**Answer:** The co-producer referred to the producer's answer about AMAP management approval. Trust and personal relationships are important, and problems would preferably be resolved informally rather than through a formal dispute process. An in-platform chat would be useful, and AMAP management could also help. No specific renewal procedure was described.

### 4. Membership and waiting lists
**Question:** How should new members be admitted and waiting lists prioritized? Would you also participate as a producer or coordinator, and what would you need to manage?

**Answer:** No direct response to admission, waiting-list priority or taking on producer/coordinator roles was recorded. The co-producer separately suggested that people should be able to explore an AMAP before joining, including seeing what it produces and what subscription options are available. A person can enroll in multiple AMAPs.

### 5. Collection arrangements and notifications
**Question:** If you cannot collect your basket, how would you arrange collection by someone else or choose what happens to it? Which reminders and updates would you want, and through which channels?

**Answer:** No direct co-producer response was recorded.

### 6. Internationalization
**Question:** Which languages, currencies and countries do you need amaply to support at launch?

**Answer:** Portuguese and English are the most important languages. Supporting other languages would be useful if the platform is well designed. No specific countries or currencies were named.

## Additional Interview Findings

- AMAPs should be listed geographically because co-producers are likely to choose nearby communities.
- AMAPs may communicate with one another, but this is not necessarily required.
- Each AMAP should be able to decide whether to use payments through the platform or externally.
- AMAPs can provide multiple delivery or collection locations to attract more co-producers.
- Co-producers would benefit from being able to browse an AMAP's products and options before joining.
- Co-producers can participate in multiple AMAPs.
